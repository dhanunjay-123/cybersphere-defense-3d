S_final = 0.28·S_rule + 0.32·S_ML + 0.15·S_path + 0.25·S_decoy

Weights are empirically calibrated to optimize ROC-AUC while preserving zero false positive alerts during regular user office work.

Measures data encryption randomness; values above 7.85 bits/byte trigger immediate heuristic escalation.
